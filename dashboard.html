Basics First
Figure out if you need to file, which form applies, and when it's due.
Your income is above the basic exemption limit
₹4 lakh (new regime) / ₹2.5 lakh (old regime) for individuals under 60 — filing is mandatory regardless of tax payable.
TDS was deducted on your income
By your employer, bank, or client — you can claim a refund only by filing a return.
You need it for loans, credit cards, or visas
Banks and embassies commonly ask for ITR as proof of income and financial credibility.
You have foreign assets, income, or signing authority abroad
Filing is mandatory in this case even if your income is below the exemption limit.
You want to carry forward a loss
Capital or business losses can only be carried forward if the original return is filed on time.
Not sure where you stand? Book a slot and we'll confirm together.
Do you have income from a business or profession?
Do you file under the presumptive taxation scheme (Section 44AD / 44ADA)?
This applies if your accountant/previous filer mentioned "presumptive income" — common for small businesses/freelancers with turnover under the prescribed limit.
Do any of these apply to you?
Capital gains (property/stocks/mutual funds) · foreign income or assets · more than two house properties · total income above ₹50 lakh
