Welcome
Here's how filethisitr.com works, step by step.
1. Figure out your ITR form type
Not sure which form applies to you? Check the "Which Form?" tab for guidance.
2. Book a free slot
Head to "Book a Slot" — choose ITR filing or tax advisory, pick a date (and time, for ITR filing).
3. Chat with me
Click your booking under "My Bookings" to message me directly — questions, updates, anything.
4. Upload your documents
Form 16, PAN, and anything else needed — under "Upload Documents", tied to your booking.
5. Pay once agreed
Once the amount is confirmed in chat, pay directly from "My Bookings" or the "Payments" tab.
